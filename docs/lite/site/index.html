What Lite does

Built for messy reality.

01

Normalize

Heterogeneous JSON, text and nested attributes map to canonical events. Source data stays intact.

02

Quality

Missing, inferred, unmapped and conflicting fields remain visible—not silently fabricated.

03

Correlate

Trace, request, parent-span and business-entity links remain distinct; ambiguous joins are challenged.

04

Detect

Deterministic signals for failures, latency, retries, severity mismatch and invariants.

05

Reason

Evidence-linked incident hypotheses with alternatives and uncertainty—not certainty theater.

↻

Recover

Durable work, leases, retries, idempotency and replay support worker interruptions.
